A critter whose footing a door just took away falls at once, instead of standing there until it realizes it should be falling.

[list]
[*]Pez droppers fire the moment the door has closed on the critter.
[*]With Sgt_Imalas's [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3816086407]Critters fall through open doors[/url] installed, critters drop the moment a door opens under them (or, for climbers, above them).
[*]Duplicants and robots are not affected.
[/list]

Does nothing unless a door just changed state, so there is no cost the rest of the time. Checked against Fast Track.

Source and issues: https://github.com/isochronous/fall-you-bastard
