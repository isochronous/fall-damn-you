Critters fall through open doors.

[list]
[*]A critter standing on a door falls through the moment the door is opened, by hand or by automation.
[*]Critters no longer walk across open doors.
[*]Closed and locked doors still carry critters, and so do automatic doors that only opened for a duplicant.
[*]A critter whose floor a door just took away, by opening under it or by closing on it, starts falling at once instead of standing there until it realizes it should be falling.
[*]Duplicants and robots are not affected.
[/list]

Both behaviours are options, on by default. Checked against Fast Track.

Source and issues: https://github.com/isochronous/fall-damn-you
